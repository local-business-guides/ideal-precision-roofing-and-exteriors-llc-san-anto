The guide offers practical points for people deciding what to ask a roofing contractor and what information to review. That can include discussing a roof leak or storm damage, understanding whether repair or replacement may be an option, and looking over photos and a written estimate. These details can help property owners prepare for a conversation about the work their roof may need.

The information may be useful when a property owner spots a leak, is concerned about storm damage or wants to compare possible next steps.
